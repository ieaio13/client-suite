Features

Everything an agency needs.
Nothing it doesn’t.

Built for social and content teams who juggle many brands, many approvals and many invoices.

Calendar, board & table

Drag posts between days and stages. Filter by platform, tag or teammate in a click.

Client portal links

One secret link per brand. Choose “view & approve” or let clients add and edit their own posts.

One-tap approvals

Clients approve or request changes with a note. You get an email the moment they do.

Tags & color-coded clients

A reusable tag library and a color for every client, in the order you choose.

Invoices & PDFs

Line items, tax and discounts. Generate a PDF and email it to the client in one go.

Secure by default

Role-based access for your team and a 6-digit email code on every new device.

How it works

From idea to invoice in three steps

1

Plan the content

Add clients and brands, then fill the calendar with posts, media and tags.

2

Share for review

Send each brand its portal link. Clients comment, approve, or add posts of their own.

3

Publish & bill

Track what went live, log results, and send the month’s invoice as a PDF.

Bring your clients into the flow

Sign in to your workspace and share your first portal link in under a minute.
